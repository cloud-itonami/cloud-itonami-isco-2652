# Business Model: Independent Music Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-2652`
- ISCO-08: `2652`
- Occupation: Musicians, Singers and Composers
- Social impact: creative-livelihoods, cultural-expression, data-sovereignty

## Customer

- video / anime / game producers needing BGM
- channels and studios with recurring soundtrack needs
- direct listeners (releases, live session recordings)

## Offer

- composition and BGM commissions
- catalog licensing (render-only background use)
- session performance and recording
- mixing / mastering packages

## Revenue

- per-track commission fee
- catalog licensing subscription
- usage royalties
- release sales

## Trust Controls

- render-only licensing by default: no raw public file exposure
- AI-training use and Content ID registration are refused without an
  explicit, human-signed license
- every licensed use carries credit text and provenance
- usage and licensing records are auditable, not editable
